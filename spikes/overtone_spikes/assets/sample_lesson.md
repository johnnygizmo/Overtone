# Building a Major Triad

A **major triad** contains a *root*, a *major third*, and a *perfect fifth*.
See [[understanding-pitch]] and [[understanding-intervals|intervals]] first.

> [!note] Remember
> A major third is **four** semitones; a minor third is **three**.

| Interval | Semitones |
|---|---|
| Major third | 4 |
| Perfect fifth | 7 |

1. Pick a root.
2. Add a major third above it.
3. Add a perfect fifth above the root.
